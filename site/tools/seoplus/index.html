How long does the audit take?

About 30 seconds. You paste your URL, sign in in 5 seconds (Google or email), and the analysis runs live before your eyes. Signing in is free: it archives your report in your history so you can reopen it whenever you want.

What kinds of websites can you audit?

Any public website: WordPress, Wix, Shopify, Webflow, custom sites. The analysis covers your homepage, up to 30 key pages found through your sitemap or menu, and domain signals (DNS, security, indexing files).

What exactly is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization: your visibility in AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). The audit devotes 23 checks to it: AI crawler access, llms.txt in the format Google validates, organization schema, structured data syntax, question-style content, citable passages, quantified facts, E-E-A-T signals, RSS feed, machine-readable contact path, cited sources and hidden instructions aimed at AIs.

How do the generated files work?

The full audit detects what is missing on your site (robots.txt, sitemap, llms.txt, structured data, security headers, SPF, DMARC) and generates each file with your domain and your information already in place. You copy, you paste in the right spot, done.

What if the analysis fails?

The audit costs you nothing: if your site is unreachable, try again later. If our crawler is blocked by your firewall, the report tells you exactly what to allow.

Who applies the recommendations?

You, your webmaster, or us. The generated files can be deployed without technical skills. For the rest, OptimizIA.xyz offers full handling of the fixes, with a re-audit to measure progress.
