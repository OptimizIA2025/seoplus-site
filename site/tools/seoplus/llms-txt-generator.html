Generate your llms.txt file in 2 minutes.

The llms.txt is a text file placed at the root of your website that summarizes your business for AIs (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity). It reduces errors when an AI talks about you and increases your chances of being cited correctly. Fill in, copy, deploy.

/llms.txt 

        


        

          
What next?

          
    
            
  1. Place the llms.txt file at the root of your website, next to your robots.txt (it must respond at yoursite.com/llms.txt).
    2. 
            
  2. Add the line # llms.txt : https://yourwebsite.com/llms.txt at the end of your robots.txt to help crawlers find it.
    3. 
            
  3. Keep it up to date: an llms.txt that lies (old prices, discontinued offers) does more harm than no file at all.
