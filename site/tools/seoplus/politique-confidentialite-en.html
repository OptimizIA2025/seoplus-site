Data collected

  • Website analysis (free diagnostic and Full Audit): signing in is required before the analysis starts. We then collect your email address and, if you sign in via Google, your name as provided by Google. You may also enter your company name (optional). We associate with your account the list of websites you have audited, their score, the date, and the content of each report so we can show it back to you in your history. On the engine side: every request triggers a real, immediate measurement with no caching of the result; your IP address is used solely to limit the number of analyses per day (kept for the current day).
  • In the event of a purchase: payment data is processed directly by Stripe. No banking data passes through our servers.
  • Public leaderboard (optional): if the "Show my website on the public leaderboard" box is checked when launching the analysis (you can uncheck it at any time before analyzing), your website's domain name and its measured score are published on the leaderboard page. Every registered website is displayed, whatever its score: a low score is not hidden, it is a measured starting point. Removal is available on simple email request.
  • Product feedback (account area): if you send us feedback (idea, bug, other) from your account, we keep the text of that feedback associated with your account, solely to improve the service.
  • Campaign source: if you arrive from one of our campaign links (utm_ parameters in the address), we keep that source alongside the audit you run, so we know which posts bring visitors. This is internal measurement: no advertising cookie, no third-party analytics tool, no data passed to an ad network.
  • Testimonials (account area, optional): if you submit a testimonial, we keep its text, the rating you gave, and the name and company you entered yourself. A testimonial is published only if you ticked the publishing permission, and after we have read it over. You may withdraw it at any time from your account.
  • Audit follow-up emails: after a Full Audit we write to you at most twice: a request for feedback three days later, and an invitation to re-audit your website one month after your latest audit. We keep a record of these sends (email type, date, audit concerned) precisely so that we never write to you twice for the same audit. Each email carries a one-click unsubscribe link, no sign-in needed, which stops both at once.
  • "Tips SEO" newsletter (opt-in only): if you sign up via the dedicated form, we keep your email address to send you a weekly email of SEO tips. Every email contains an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing takes effect immediately.

We collect nothing else: no advertising tracking, no resale of data, no profiling.

Cookies and local storage

SEOPlus! sets no advertising cookies. Your browser stores locally (localStorage) your sign-in session and access to your audits, on your device only.

Audience measurement

The website uses in-house audience measurement, built and hosted by OptimizIA.xyz, with no third-party tool. It runs without a consent banner because it meets the four exemption conditions set by the French data protection authority (CNIL): purpose strictly limited to audience measurement, aggregated results, no third-party recipient, no tracking across websites.

Your browser keeps a random visit identifier locally (localStorage), which contains neither your name, email nor IP address, and is not matched with any account. That identifier does not make you anonymous but pseudonymous: it does not say who you are, it tells one visit apart from another. The measured events (page views, clicks on main buttons, reading depth, time spent, campaign source) are sent to our own automation server (n8n, hosted by us) and are used solely to understand how the website is used. No data is shared with third parties or used for advertising.

You can object at any time: add ?notrack=1 to the address of any page, and measurement is durably disabled on your browser. You can also request the deletion of the events tied to your visit at contact@optimizia.xyz.

Session replay (Microsoft Clarity)

We use Microsoft Clarity to replay browsing sessions and produce heatmaps, so we can understand where visitors get stuck. Session replay does not fall under the regime of aggregated audience measurement: no exemption applies to it, so it is subject to your consent.

A banner is shown on your first visit. Until you accept, nothing is loaded and no cookie is set. After acceptance, two first-party cookies are set, and only those two: _clck and _clsk. The advertising signal is passed as denied, so no Microsoft advertising cookie is set. You can change your mind at any time through the “Manage cookies” link in the footer; declining erases the cookies already set and reloads the page to stop collection in progress.

The service is provided by Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited and hosted on Azure. Transfers to Microsoft Corporation outside the European Union are governed by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Retention is tiered: recordings are kept for 30 days, then a residual sample (1% of recordings or 10 per day, whichever is greater), flagged sessions and heatmaps are kept for up to 9 months. The content of form fields is masked at source by Clarity, and that masking cannot be turned off.
